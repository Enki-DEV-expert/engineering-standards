# Community code of conduct

We welcome constructive participation regardless of background, identity, experience or ability. Discuss engineering decisions with evidence and respect for the people involved.

Participants should be considerate, accept constructive feedback, acknowledge uncertainty and protect others' privacy. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, deliberate disruption and disclosure of private information are unacceptable.

This policy applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions and spaces where a participant represents the project. It does not restrict respectful technical disagreement.

**Nirox Morais** ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nirox-morais-87784b1ba/)), project author and maintainer under the GitHub account **Enki-DEV-expert**, is responsible for enforcement. Depending on severity and context, responses may include a request to stop, content removal, a warning, a temporary participation restriction or exclusion. Reports will be considered fairly and with attention to privacy; absolute confidentiality cannot be promised where legal or platform obligations apply.
